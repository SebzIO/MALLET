Textures by Getridou
====================

The 170 materials in the "Getridou" category (folders getridou_*) are by Getridou, who made them himself and gave
MALLET's author permission to ship them in MALLET, with credit. Thank you, Getridou! A few (Dplate, Knitted, Gumrock)
he made from photos his friends arcabal, azzie and cynthia sent him.

  Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/getridou/
  Discord:   Getridou

From the pack's own README: "Content within is considered royalty free under the condition that no profit is made
through its use" (CC-BY-SA). MALLET is free; please credit Getridou if you use these textures in your maps, and don't
sell them.

What MALLET did to them: converted from Cube 2: Sauerbraten's format (diffuse / normal / specular / glow PNGs) to
MALLET's library (colour, normal, roughness and glow JPGs, at most 1024 px), with Sauerbraten's own texture scale.
Height (parallax), ambient-occlusion and environment-map settings aren't used.
